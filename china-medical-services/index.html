How It Works

Our Medical Travel Process

Process

  1. Share your case

    Send medical reports and your goals through our secure inquiry.

  2. Hospital opinion

  3. Plan & visa

    Confirm dates, receive invitation letters and arrange the visa.

  4. Travel & treatment

  5. Return & follow-up

Please note: TAREEK AL BAHAR TOURS LLC is a travel and coordination service. We do not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. All medical decisions are made between you and your doctors.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a special visa for medical treatment in China?

Most patients travel on a visa suitable for their situation. We guide you on the correct type and prepare the supporting documents and hospital invitation.

Can a family member travel with me?

Yes. We arrange the visa, accommodation and transfers for one or more companions.

How much does treatment cost?

Costs vary by hospital, condition and treatment plan. After reviewing your records we request an estimate from the hospital and share it with you before you commit.

Will there be a language barrier?

We provide interpreters for consultations, admissions and daily needs so communication is never a concern.
